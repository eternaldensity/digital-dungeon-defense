> An unknown amount of time after humanity merged cyberspace and reality, a being experienced solidity. It found itself in a pocket of stability, surrounded by the chaos of everything.  Creating or upholding this pocket was a core, pulsating with energy. Was the being within the core? Or was the being itself the core? You are unsure. All you know is that you must protect the core from any that seek its power. For what greater prize is there in the void of chaos than the ability to enforce solid reality?

You control the core of a digital dungeon.
Waves of various digital creatures arrive in the dungeon, seeking the core. You must stop them with traps and by spawning digital creatures (called FloofBytes) of your own to fight them.
You have some control over the direction of your dungeon bubble as it floats through the chaos of cybereality. That is to say, you have some choice as to the upcoming waves of creatures, as is common in roguelites.

Every few waves you encounter an adventurer-hacker or a group of them. Their strength (and number of chips attainable) depends on the amount of treasure you've made available to be looted.

The core naturally collects **bytes**.
The rate is determined by the level of the core. Storage can be upgraded and external storage is available.
Bytes are used to reset and repair dungeon furnishings after they've been deployed or damaged. This includes activating spawners.

The core gathers **floof** from foes that are harmed within the dungeon.
Floof is used to construct dungeon rooms and furnishings. Furnishings include traps, spawners, and chests. Floof is also used as a cost to apply permanent modifiers to creatures. 

**Chips** are a special currency dropped by intelligent invaders on defeat.
Chips are used to add levels to the dungeon. The core's level is determined by the number of the dungeon level it is placed on.

1. **Build the Digital Dungeon:** Place traps, summon FloofBytes, and upgrade defenses to attract and repel invaders.
2. **Defend Against Hackers:** Manage real-time tower defense-style battles against waves of hackers and rogue programs.
3. **Train and Evolve FloofBytes:** Use energy gained from defeated invaders to strengthen your creatures, unlocking new abilities and forms.
4. **Raid Enemy Dungeons:** Send FloofBytes to infiltrate rival servers, overcoming their defenses in hacking battles to claim rare loot and resources.

Three consecutive floors of the dungeon are active at a time during combat situations. If an attacker wishes to progress to a further floor and has units left behind, these will be lost. This prevents players from having too much activity to process at a time.

When the player adds new floors they choose a floor type, which grants modifiers to that floor and to adjacent floors.

Each creature spawner creates a limited number of creatures, which have a limited lifetime. The same individuals get respawned by the spawner after death/timeout. They individually gain experience from engaging in combat and especially from surviving combat. The kinds of combat they're in and types of enemies they encounter shapes what upgrades can be resarched by the dungeon core.
